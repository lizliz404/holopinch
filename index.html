HoloPinch

What

Hold light between your fingers. A live hologram mesh — camera in the browser. No app, no account, no backend.

Play

The camera wakes on its own — pinch or spread one or both hands. Closer + flat → foil card. Farther + open → crystal bar. Best with two hands. No camera, or denied? The demo orbs on stage already respond; open controls for more.

Angles

Angles stay off by default so the light stays primary. Open controls → Angles / Guess when you want the STEM layer.

Tech

MediaPipe HandLandmarker + WebGL + three.js. One flatness value drives opacity, edges, and foil.
