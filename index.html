HoloPinch

What

Hold light between your fingers. A live hologram mesh — camera in the browser. No app, no account, no backend.

Play

Camera starts itself. Show one or both hands; pinch or spread. Closer + flat → foil card. Farther + open → crystal bar. Best with two hands. No camera? Open controls → Demo, drag the orbs.

Angles

Turn on Angles / Guess to read or guess the pinch quad’s interior angles.

Tech

MediaPipe HandLandmarker + WebGL + three.js. One flatness value drives opacity, edges, and foil.
