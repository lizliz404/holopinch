About HoloPinch

What it is

HoloPinch is a browser AR toy. Pinch the air with both hands and a living holographic solid appears between them — hand tracking via MediaPipe, rendered in WebGL. No app install, no account, no backend.

How to play

Start camera → show both hands → pinch thumb+index. Move hands apart and spread fingers to grow a translucent crystal bar; bring them close and flat for an opaque foil card. No camera? Demo mode: drag the orbs (touch: two-finger gestures).

The angle game

Toggle Angles/Guess to guess the interior angles of your pinch quad.

Tech

Vite + TypeScript + three.js + MediaPipe HandLandmarker. One continuous flatness scalar drives opacity, edges, and film — no mode switch.
